# About Me

Hello, I'm Dhia Chebbi, a Computer Engineering student at ESPRIT and a graduate of ISET Tozeur in Information Technology.

My journey in technology began with a simple curiosity: understanding how digital systems work behind the scenes. Over time, that curiosity evolved into a passion for designing and building software solutions that solve real-world problems. Whether developing enterprise applications, healthcare platforms, or mobile solutions, I enjoy transforming ideas into reliable, user-centered products.

Through academic projects, internships, and personal initiatives, I have gained hands-on experience across the entire software development lifecycle—from requirements analysis and UML modeling to development, testing, and deployment. These experiences have strengthened both my technical expertise and my ability to collaborate within multidisciplinary teams.

Among the projects I am most proud of are FlexDesk, a full-stack workspace management platform developed during my internship at Sofrecom Tunisia, BioSync, a healthcare management platform designed to improve patient care and medical follow-up, and several mobile and web applications focused on productivity, finance, and digital services.

Currently pursuing an Engineering Degree at ESPRIT, I am continuously expanding my technical skills while seeking opportunities to contribute to innovative projects, tackle challenging problems, and create technology that delivers meaningful impact.
